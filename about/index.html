About Sound of Text

Free, open text-to-speech for everyone

Sound of Text is a free text-to-speech platform that lets you convert written text into natural-sounding audio instantly — no account, no subscription, no voice recording equipment needed. Whether you're a content creator, language learner, educator, or someone who simply wants to hear text read aloud, Sound of Text makes it effortless.

Our Story

Sound of Text was originally pioneered by NC Pierson as an open-source project, and has grown through the Soundtext.org community into one of the most accessible TTS tools on the web. The Sound of Text Com mobile application, developed by Appz, extends this mission to Android devices with advanced features like 1-step auto-translation, bulk text import, and audio file merging.

What We Offer

Web — Live Text-to-Speech Studio

Our browser-based tool uses the Web Speech API to generate speech entirely on-device. No audio data is uploaded to any server. You can adjust voice, speed, pitch, and volume, and watch real-time word-by-word karaoke highlighting as the text is read aloud.

Mobile — Sound of Text Com App

The free Android app takes things further with:

  • 50+ Global Languages — far beyond what most browsers offer natively.
  • 1-Step Auto-Translate & Voice — type in your language, get audio in another.
  • Bulk Text Import & Audio Merge — paste multiple lines and combine them into a single audio file.
  • Offline History — all your generated audio is saved locally on your device.
  • No Sign-Up Required — download, open, and start converting immediately.

Our Mission

We believe that converting text to speech should be free, fast, and private. Our tools are built for accessibility — helping people who need audio versions of text for learning, creating content, or personal convenience, without barriers or paywalls.
