Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2025

Thank you for using the Sound of Text Com mobile application ("Application"), developed by Appz. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or account credentials. Text-to-speech rendering requests are processed anonymously.

Non-Personal Information

To help us maintain and improve the Application's stability, performance, and user experience, we may collect non-personal, aggregated device and performance metrics:

  • Device Characteristics: Device model, operating system version, and system language settings.
  • App Performance Logs: Crash reports, loading speed, and diagnostic analytics.
  • App Usage Statistics: Anonymized metrics detailing feature interaction.

2. Permissions Declared & Data Access

The Application requests the following permissions to carry out its core operations. These permissions are used exclusively to process your requests on-device and are not used to collect user data:

  • Internet/Network Access: Required to convert text to speech audio clips and load advertisements.
  • Access Network State: Used to verify network connectivity before fetching audio files.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

We partner with third-party networks for ad delivery and app analytics. These services may access your mobile advertising identifier (such as the Google Advertising ID) in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Provides backend library functionality and application integrity checks. Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • AdMob (Google): Used to deliver advertising banners and interstitials. AdMob Privacy Policy & Opt-Out · Google Partner Sites Data Policy

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because the Application does not utilize accounts or cloud databases:

  • We do not host your entered text inputs, generated audio files, or user logs on our servers.
  • All configuration data and local download histories are saved locally on your device. You can erase this data at any time by clearing the Application cache or by uninstalling the Application from your device.
  • Any support messages sent to dev@appz.se are kept confidential. You can request the permanent deletion of your email contact history at any time.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The Application is not intended for or directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has sent us a support request, we will delete that information immediately. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has contacted us, please notify us at dev@appz.se.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect anonymous usage statistics. However, please note that no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Modifications will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We recommend checking this page regularly to stay informed.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Developer: Appz
  • Contact Email: dev@appz.se
  • Official Website: https://help.appz.se
