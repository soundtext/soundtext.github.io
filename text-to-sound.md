## Overview

Transform written text into clear sound clips. Designed for creators who need quick voice soundbites for videos, presentations, or audio projects.

### Use Cases

* **Video Editing**: Generate quick voice soundbites for YouTube shorts, TikTok, and Reels.
* **Audio Notifications**: Create custom sound alerts for apps and desktop notifications.
* **Accessibility**: Turn written notes and prompts into audible sound files.

### Steps to Generate Sound Files

1. Enter your text or phrase in the tool above.
2. Select your preferred audio language.
3. Click **Convert to Speech** and download your MP3 sound clip instantly.
