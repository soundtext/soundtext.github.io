# Best Sound of Text Voices for WhatsApp Ringtones

Hearing the same old WhatsApp ringtones can get boring sometimes. Luckily, with “Sound of Text,” you can create your own ringtones using unique voices, from anime characters to robotic voices. Here are the five most popular “Sound of Text” voices used for WhatsApp ringtones.

## 1. Anime Character Voices

![Sound of Text Anime](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/sound-of-text-anime.jpg)

The most exciting option for anime fans. Voices like Naruto, Luffy, Levi, or Doraemon can be made to say short phrases like “You’ve got a new message!” or “Hey! Check your phone!” Perfect for those who want a ringtone that feels personal while also evoking nostalgia for their favorite anime.

## 2. Baby Voice

![Sound of Text Baby Voice](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/sound-of-text-baby-voice.jpg)

Perfect if you want a ringtone that’s funny, cute, and different from most people’s. Simple text like “You’ve got a message!” can sound much more unique with a baby voice, especially when set as a notification for family groups or close friends.

## 3. Robot / AI Voice

![Sound of Text Robot Voice](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/sound-of-text-robot-voice.jpg)

A great choice for a futuristic and modern vibe. For example: “New message received,” “You have an important message,” or “Someone is looking for you.” Perfect for those who love a tech-savvy feel and don’t want a ringtone that’s too loud or funny.

## 4. Movie & Cartoon Character Voices

![Sound of Text Characters](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/sound-of-text-characters.jpg)

Voices like the Minions, Darth Vader, Batman, Mickey Mouse, or Donald Duck can make for more playful ringtones. Characters with very distinctive voices are usually easier to recognize as notifications, so you’ll know right away that it’s WhatsApp ringing without having to look at the screen.

## 5. Google Voice with Your Own Name

![Sound of Text Call Your Name](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/sound-of-text-call-your-name.jpg)

This is perfect for personalization. For example: “[Your Name], you have a new message.” or “Hey [Your Name], don’t forget to check WhatsApp.” This format also feels like a notification just for you, since your name is mentioned directly every time a message comes in.

## How to Set “Sound of Text” as Your WhatsApp Ringtone

![How to Change Your WhatsApp Ringtone](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/how-to-change-your-whatsapp-ringtone.jpg)

After selecting and creating an MP3 file from one of the sound types above, the next step is to set it as your WhatsApp ringtone. Here’s how:

- Open the WhatsApp app on your device
- Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner
- Then scroll down and select the Settings menu
- In the Settings menu, scroll down until you find the Notifications menu
- Once there, select Notification Sound at the bottom of the screen
- The screen will display a list of ringtones. From here, you can click the plus (+) button or the “Add” button
- Continue by selecting the MP3 audio file you want to use
- Now click the “Set” button. Your WhatsApp ringtone will be changed to the selected MP3 audio file

From the five options above, you can customize them to match your own style and personality. Whether you want something funny, personal, or futuristic, you can easily create it all using the text-to-speech websites discussed earlier. Good luck!
