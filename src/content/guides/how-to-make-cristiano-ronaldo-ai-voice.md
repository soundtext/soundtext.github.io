## How to Set Cristiano Ronaldo’s AI Voice as a WhatsApp Ringtone

![How to Change Your WhatsApp Ringtone](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/how-to-change-your-whatsapp-ringtone.jpg)

An MP3 file of Cristiano Ronaldo’s voice that’s already saved on your smartphone can be used as a WhatsApp ringtone. If you’re still unsure, follow this tutorial:

- Open the WhatsApp app on your device.
- Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner.
- Then scroll down and select the **Settings** menu.
- In the Settings menu, scroll down until you find the **Notifications** menu.
- Once there, select **Notification Sound** under the Messages section.
- The screen will display a list of ringtones. Tap the plus (+) button or **Add**.
- Select the Cristiano Ronaldo MP3 audio file you want to use.
- Now click the **Set** button. Your WhatsApp ringtone will be changed to the selected MP3 audio file.
