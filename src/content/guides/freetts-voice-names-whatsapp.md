# FreeTTS Voice Names WhatsApp

Text-to-speech has long been a favorite way to create WhatsApp ringtones that stand out from the rest. One of the most sought-after variations is a text-to-speech message that announces the sender’s name, so you know right away who’s messaging you without having to pick up your phone.

There are several websites you can use to create these text-to-speech messages, ranging from FreeTTS and Myinstants to FakeYou. Here’s how to create them, one by one.

## 1. Creating “Sound of Text” with FreeTTS

![FreeTTS](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/freetts-com.jpg)

[FreeTTS.com](https://freetts.com/) is one of the most popular free text-to-speech websites used to create “Sound of Text” for WhatsApp. The process is quite simple and doesn’t require you to sign up for an account.

- Visit freetts.com in your browser
- Click the “Start Text to Speech Free” button
- Type your script into the text box, for example: “Hello, there’s a WhatsApp message for (your name)”
- Click “Languages” to select Indonesian
- Simply click the “Convert to MP3” button once
- Click “Download MP3” to save it

## 2. Creating Text-to-Speech with FakeYou

![FakeYou](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/fakeyou-com.jpg)

[FakeYou](https://fakeyou.com/) is a text-to-speech website that offers a wide selection of voices, including those of famous characters and personalities. The process of creating text-to-speech here is a bit longer than with FreeTTS, but the results are more varied.

- Open the TTS website, in this case, fakeyou.com, in your browser
- Type the sentence or words you want to convert to speech into the text field provided
- Click “Speak” and listen to the result
- If you’re satisfied, click the “Share & Download” link
- Click “Download file,” select the three-dot icon on the recorder, and choose “Download”
- Save the audio file to your desired location or transfer it to your phone

## 3. Creating “Sound of Text” with Myinstants

![Myinstants](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/myinstants-com.jpg)

Unlike FreeTTS and FakeYou, [Myinstants](https://www.myinstants.com/) isn’t a text-to-speech website, but rather a collection of ready-made voices and instant memes. It’s perfect for those who want funny ringtones without having to write their own scripts.

- Visit myinstants.com in your browser
- Select and click on the colorful buttons to play the sounds instantly
- Use the search bar at the top to find specific sounds or memes
- Download the audio if you want to use it for video editing

## How to Set “Sound of Text” as a WhatsApp Ringtone

![How to Change Your WhatsApp Ringtone](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/how-to-change-your-whatsapp-ringtone.jpg)

Once the MP3 file has been successfully downloaded, the next step is to set it as a WhatsApp ringtone. Here’s how:

- Open the WhatsApp app on your device
- Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner
- Then scroll down and select the Settings menu
- In the Settings menu, scroll down until you find the Notifications menu
- Once there, select Notification Sound at the bottom of the screen
- The screen will display a list of ringtones. From there, click the plus (+) button or the “Add” button
- Select the MP3 audio file you want to use
- Now click the “Set” button. Your WhatsApp ringtone will be changed to the selected MP3 audio file

With the three options listed above, you’re free to choose the one that suits you best. Whether you prefer ones that let you customize your own scripts, like FreeTTS and FakeYou, or ones that let you use pre-made voices right away, like Myinstants. Give them a try!
