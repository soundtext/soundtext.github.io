# How to Make SpongeBob Voice Text to Speech

SpongeBob’s voice is definitely one of the most sought-after for creating funny content, from TikTok video voice-overs to hilarious WhatsApp ringtones. If you’re curious about how to make them, here’s a complete tutorial using FakeYou.

## How to Create a SpongeBob Voice Using FakeYou

![SpongeBob Voice in FakeYou](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/spongebob-voice-in-fakeyou.jpg)

FakeYou is a text-to-speech website that offers a wide selection of character voices, including SpongeBob SquarePants. Just type in the sentence you want, and the site will automatically convert it into SpongeBob’s voice. Here are the steps:

- Open the TTS website, [fakeyou.com](https://fakeyou.com/weight/weight_5by9kjm8vr8xsp7abe8zvaxc8/spongebob-squarepants), in your browser.

![Type Your Script in the Text Blocks](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/type-the-script-you-wanted-to-be-sounded-like-spongebob.jpg)

- Type the sentence or words you want to convert into speech in the text field provided.
- Click **“Speak”** and listen to the result.

![Choose Share & Download](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/choose-share-and-download.jpg)

- If you’re satisfied, click the **“Share & Download”** link.

![Click Download file](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/click-download-file.jpg)

- Click **“Download file”**, select the three-dot icon on the recorder, and choose **“Download”**.
- Save the audio file wherever you like, or transfer it to your phone.

## How to Set a SpongeBob Sound as Your WhatsApp Ringtone

![Change the Ringtone on WhatsApp](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/change-the-ringtone-on-whatsapp.jpg)

Once you’ve successfully downloaded the SpongeBob MP3 file, you can set it as your WhatsApp ringtone right away. Here’s how:

- Open the WhatsApp app on your device.
- Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner.
- Then scroll down and select the **Settings** menu.
- In the Settings menu, scroll down until you find the **Notifications** menu.
- Once there, select **Notification Sound** at the bottom of the screen.
- The screen will display a list of ringtones. From there, click the plus (+) button or **Add**.
- Next, select the MP3 audio file you want to use.
- Now click the **Set** button. Your WhatsApp ringtone will be changed to the selected MP3 audio file.

By following the steps above, you can easily create your own SpongeBob sound and set it as a unique ringtone on WhatsApp. Give it a try!
