## How to Set Bruce Buffer’s Voice as a WhatsApp Ringtone

![Change the Ringtone on WhatsApp](https://cdn.emzeth.com/sot/change-the-ringtone-on-whatsapp.jpg)

Once you’ve successfully downloaded the Bruce Buffer MP3 file, you can set it as your WhatsApp ringtone right away. Here’s how:

- Open the WhatsApp app on your device.
- Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner.
- Then scroll down and select the **Settings** menu.
- In the Settings menu, scroll down until you find the **Notifications** menu.
- Once there, select **Notification Sound** at the bottom of the screen.
- The screen will display a list of ringtones. Here, you can click the plus (+) button or **Add**.
- Continue by selecting one of the MP3 audio files you want to use.
- Now click the **Set** button. Your WhatsApp ringtone will be changed to the selected MP3 audio file.
