How to Create & Set Custom WhatsApp Ringtones

  1. Type your desired custom text into the text area above or visit our homepage tool.
  2. Select your target language from the dropdown selector.
  3. Click Convert to Speech and listen to the audio preview.
  4. Click Download MP3 to save the audio file to your device.
  5. On your phone, open WhatsApp > Settings > Notifications > select your downloaded MP3 audio file.

FAQ

What is Sound of Text?

Sound of Text is a free online Text-to-Speech (TTS) tool that converts written text into high-quality MP3 speech using Google Translate's speech synthesis engine.

How to create a custom WhatsApp ringtone?

Enter your text, choose your preferred language, click Convert, and download the MP3 file. Then open WhatsApp Settings > Notifications and select the MP3 sound.

Is Sound of Text free to use?

Yes, 100% free with no registration, login, or installation required.

What is the difference between the homepage and this directory?

The homepage is the full converter studio with bulk import and audio merging. This page is a directory that lists every Sound of Text voice by country so you can jump straight to one language generator.
