## Sound of Text USA (American English)

**Sound of Text USA** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* generator that converts written text into MP3 audio with a general American English accent using Google's speech engine.

The American English voice is the world's most widely used TTS accent — ideal for global content creation, e-learning narration, and custom notification sounds.

### Steps to Set a USA Voice Ringtone on WhatsApp

1. Type your text in the generator above and select **English (US)**.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and preview the audio.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the file.
4. Open **WhatsApp** > **Settings** > **Notifications** > select your downloaded MP3.
