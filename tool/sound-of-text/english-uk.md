## Sound of Text UK (British English)

**Sound of Text UK** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* generator that converts written text into MP3 audio with a refined British English accent using Google's speech engine.

Perfect for English learners who prefer the British accent, narration for documentaries and study content, and unique WhatsApp notification sounds.

---

### Key Features of Sound of Text UK

* **Authentic British Accent**: Clear Received Pronunciation style intonation, ideal for formal narration.
* **Instant MP3 Downloads**: One click to save the audio file to your phone or computer.
* **No Registration Required**: Works instantly in your browser with unlimited conversions.

### Steps to Set a UK Voice Ringtone on WhatsApp

1. Type your text in the generator above and select **English (UK)**.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and preview the audio.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the file.
4. Open **WhatsApp** > **Settings** > **Notifications** > select your downloaded MP3.
