## Sound of Text Australia (Aussie English)

**Sound of Text Australia** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* generator that converts written text into MP3 audio with an Australian English accent using Google's speech engine.

Ideal for learners of the Aussie accent, travel and lifestyle content creators, and anyone who wants a unique G'day-style notification sound.

---

### Key Features of Sound of Text Australia

* **Distinctive Aussie Accent**: Authentic Australian vowel sounds and laid-back intonation.
* **Instant MP3 Downloads**: Save your audio in one click to any device.
* **No Registration Required**: Unlimited conversions directly in your browser.
* **Compare Other Accents**: Try <a href="{{ '/sound-of-text/english-uk/' | relative_url }}">Sound of Text UK</a> or <a href="{{ '/sound-of-text/english-usa/' | relative_url }}">Sound of Text USA</a>.

### Steps to Set an Aussie Voice Ringtone on WhatsApp

1. Type your text in the generator above and select **English (Australia)**.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and preview the audio.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the file.
4. Open **WhatsApp** > **Settings** > **Notifications** > select your downloaded MP3.
