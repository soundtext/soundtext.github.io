## Sound of Text English Voice Generator

**Sound of Text English** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* generator that converts written English text into clear, natural-sounding MP3 audio using Google Translate's speech engine.

Whether you need an English voiceover for video creation, global WhatsApp notification ringtones, or pronunciation practice for learning English, this tool delivers instant, high-quality audio files.

### Steps to Set English MP3 Ringtones on WhatsApp

1. Type your desired English text in the box above.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and listen to the audio preview.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the file.
4. Open **WhatsApp** > **Settings** > **Notifications** > **Notification Tone** > select your downloaded MP3 audio.
