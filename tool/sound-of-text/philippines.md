## Sound of Text Philippines (Filipino/Tagalog)

**Sound of Text Philippines** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* generator that converts written text into MP3 audio with a natural Filipino/Tagalog voice using Google's speech engine.

Perfect for custom WhatsApp notification sounds, narration for TikTok and YouTube Shorts, and practicing Filipino pronunciation.

---

### Key Features of Sound of Text Philippines

* **Natural Filipino Voice**: Clear Tagalog pronunciation with friendly intonation.
* **Taglish Friendly**: Handles mixed Tagalog-English sentences just like daily conversation.
* **Instant MP3 Downloads**: Save your audio in one click to any device.

### Steps to Set a Filipino Voice Ringtone on WhatsApp

1. Type your text in the generator above and select **Filipino**.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and preview the audio.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the file.
4. Open **WhatsApp** > **Settings** > **Notifications** > select your downloaded MP3.
