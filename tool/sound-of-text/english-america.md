## Sound of Text America

**Sound of Text America** (part of our [Sound of Text Hub]({{ '/sound-of-text/' | relative_url }}) and [soundtext.org]({{ '/' | relative_url }})) is a free *Text-to-Speech (TTS)* tool that turns written text into natural American English MP3 audio using Google's speech engine.

Great for creators making US-audience content, students practicing American pronunciation, and anyone wanting a classic US notification sound.

---

### Key Features of Sound of Text America

* **Natural US Voice**: Smooth, broadcast-style American English pronunciation.
* **Instant MP3 Downloads**: One click to save and share your audio.
* **Free & Unlimited**: No sign-up, no installation, no hidden fees.
* **Explore More Accents**: Try <a href="{{ '/sound-of-text/english-uk/' | relative_url }}">Sound of Text UK</a>, <a href="{{ '/sound-of-text/english-usa/' | relative_url }}">Sound of Text USA</a>, or <a href="{{ '/sound-of-text/english-au/' | relative_url }}">Sound of Text Australia</a>.

### Steps to Use Sound of Text America

1. Enter your text in the generator above and select **English (US)**.
2. Click **Convert to Speech** and listen to the preview.
3. Click **Download MP3** to save the audio.
4. Set it as a WhatsApp notification via **Settings** > **Notifications**.
