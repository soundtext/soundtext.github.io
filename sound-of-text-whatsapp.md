## Overview

Convert any written message into a downloadable MP3 voice file designed for WhatsApp messaging, voice notes, and custom audio notifications.

### How to Use on WhatsApp

1. **Type Message**: Enter your text into the generator above.
2. **Select Language**: Pick your preferred language or accent.
3. **Convert & Download**: Click **Convert to Speech** and download the MP3 file.
4. **Send on WhatsApp**:
   * Open any WhatsApp chat.
   * Tap the **Attachment** icon (paperclip / plus) $\rightarrow$ select **Audio**.
   * Choose your downloaded MP3 file and hit **Send**.

### Popular Use Cases

* **Custom Ringtones**: Create personalized alert tones for specific contacts.
* **Prank & Fun Audios**: Generate funny automated voice messages for group chats.
* **Language Practice**: Send accurate voice pronunciations to language partners.
