How Can Sound of Text Help You?

Sound of Text is a free, accessible web-based Text-to-Speech tool that converts written text into natural-sounding audio. Originally created as part of soundtext.org by creator NC Pierson, our platform empowers creators, educators, and language learners worldwide.

Content Creation

Generate high-quality voiceovers for podcasts and video projects with our complete text-to-speech tool — no hardware required.

Education & Learning

Create spoken audio from study notes, textbooks, or flashcards — perfect for language practice using our voice profile options.

WhatsApp & Messaging

Craft fun, personalized voice messages for WhatsApp, Telegram, and other messaging apps. Generate WhatsApp audio notes here.

Social Media & TikTok

Create viral audio clips for TikTok videos, Instagram Reels, and Shorts using our text-to-sound generator.

Accessibility

Convert written articles, documents, and text into spoken audio for easier listening and digital accessibility.

Open Source Project

Built as an accessible open-source web application. View source code and contribution details on GitHub.
