**Effective Date:** August 7, 2026

At **Sound of Text** (`soundtext.github.io`), user privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and text-to-speech tools.

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Text Inputs**: The text you enter into our converter is processed transiently to synthesize audio files. We do not permanently store, log, or read your submitted text.
* **Audio Downloads**: Generated MP3 files are generated in real-time for instant playback and download. We do not maintain a archive or storage database of user-generated audio files.
* **Log Data & Cookies**: Standard anonymous web traffic analytics (such as browser type, operating system, and page views) may be collected to monitor site health and performance.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Our tool utilizes standard web APIs and trusted third-party services to process speech synthesis request. These services process requests statelessly to return audio output without retaining user identity.

## 3. Data Protection Guarantee

* Zero permanent retention of user text inputs.
* Zero registration or account requirements.
* No personal identity data collection.
